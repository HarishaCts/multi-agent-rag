# Northwind Billing FAQ

**Source:** Billing Knowledge Base
**Author:** Finance Team
**Date:** 2026-02-20
**Access:** public

## How am I billed?

Team edition is billed per active user per month at $49. An active user is any user who
logs in at least once during the billing period. Enterprise edition is billed annually
under a custom contract.

## Can I change plans mid-cycle?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remainder of the billing
period. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.

## What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major credit cards and, for Enterprise customers, payment by invoice with
net-30 terms. Annual Enterprise contracts can also be paid by wire transfer.

## Do you offer refunds?

Monthly plans are non-refundable. Annual Enterprise contracts may be refunded on a
prorated basis within the first 30 days of the contract term.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new workspace gets a 14-day free trial of the Team edition. No credit card
is required to start the trial. At the end of the trial the workspace reverts to the
Starter edition unless a paid plan is selected.
