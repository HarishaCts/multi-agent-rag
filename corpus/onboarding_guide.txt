Northwind Analytics - New User Onboarding Guide

Source: Onboarding Handbook
Author: Customer Success
Date: 2026-01-28
Access: public

Welcome to Northwind Analytics. This guide walks a brand-new user through their first
day on the platform.

Step 1 - Create your account
Sign up at app.northwind.example using your work email. New accounts start on the
Starter edition by default. You can upgrade to Team or Enterprise from the billing page.

Step 2 - Connect your first data source
Go to Settings > Data Sources and click "Add Source". Northwind supports PostgreSQL,
MySQL, Snowflake, BigQuery, Amazon S3, and a generic REST connector. You will need
read-only database credentials. Northwind never requires write access to your data.

Step 3 - Build your first dashboard
Click "New Dashboard", pick a data source, and drag fields onto the canvas. The first
dashboard is created from a template to help you get started quickly.

Step 4 - Invite teammates
Admins can invite teammates from Settings > Members. Invited users receive an email
and must accept within 7 days before the invite expires.

Getting help
Starter users get community support via the forum. Team users get email support with a
24-hour response target. Enterprise users get a dedicated success manager and a
1-hour response target for urgent issues.
